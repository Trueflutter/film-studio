## A. Screenplay (Fountain)

```fountain
Title: {{title}}

INT. KITCHEN - NIGHT

Rain against the window. MAYA (30s, paint on her knuckles) sits in the dark, phone face-down.

The phone buzzes. She doesn't turn it over.

MAYA
(to herself)
Not tonight.

She stands. The chair scrapes. She walks out of frame; the phone keeps buzzing on the table.
```

Rules that matter more for AI films:
- Action lines describe only what a camera can see and a microphone can hear.
- One visible action per sentence. Each sentence is a candidate shot.
- Subtext over statement: characters rarely say what they feel.
