## Insight
_The human truth, tension or desire the product meets. Not a product feature._

_The one thing we want them to take away, in one sentence._

## Product truth (reason to believe)
_The real, demonstrable fact that earns the proposition._

_What stops the thumb? A face, a motion, a question, a contradiction._

## Visual idea
_The picture that carries the proposition without words._

## Tension → payoff
_What's at stake in the spot, and how does the product resolve it?_

## CTA
