## The ladder every shot climbs
1. **Still**: composition, light, identity. Cents per try. Iterate freely.
2. **Motion draft**: action, camera, timing, performance at 480p or a fast tier. Iterate until it plays.
3. **Lock**: rate the winning draft `select` and write down why.
4. **Final**: promote the locked draft (linked draft→final) or upscale it. Only then consider a premium re-render.
5. **Fix**: repair in post (reframe, cut around, frame edit, region edit) before you regenerate.

## Stop rules
- A shot that hasn't converged after its draft budget gets **redesigned** (simpler action, different angle, split into two, insert instead), not re-rolled.
- No premium render without a `select` draft or still in `takes.csv`.
- Hero shots (≤ 20% of the film) may take 3× the average budget. Everything else is edited to hide.
