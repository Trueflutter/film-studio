## Shot card (the direction, in plain language)
- **Purpose:** what the audience must understand or feel
- **Subject + one action:**
- **Frame:** size, angle, lens, composition (where things sit in frame)
- **Camera:** one move, and what motivates it
- **Light:** source, direction, quality, temperature
- **Performance:** the feeling and how strong, its physical signs, where it lives, when each change happens; where the eyes look
- **Sound:** what we hear (even if the model is silent)
- **Continuity locks:** character anchor line, wardrobe, location lock line, screen direction

## Per shot (anything with a person or several cuts: `seedance-directing`)
- **Optics:** focal-length look, depth of field
- **Camera:** size, angle and side (faces THREE-QUARTER), the rig and the unseen operator, the move and its cause
- **Action:** the one action, screen direction, the first-frame state
- **Timing:** windows of tenths of a second for each beat, each with an end; cuts on whole seconds
- **Audio:** this shot's own sounds
- **Lock:** the one thing this shot gets wrong most often
