## Reference pack (in `refs/`)

Keep the sheets boring: neutral grey background, flat even light, real skin texture,
no grain, no cinematic grade. The look belongs to the shot, not to the reference.

- [ ] `face_34.png`: face close-up, 3/4 view, neutral expression (the master identity image)
- [ ] `face_front.png`, `face_profile.png`
- [ ] `body_front.png`, `body_back.png`: full body, head cropped out or tiny (so the model doesn't take the face from here)
- [ ] `expressions.png`: 4-6 expressions this character actually needs in the script
- [ ] `wardrobe_<scene>.png`: one per costume state
- [ ] (optional) `turnaround/`: burst-frame orbit frames, or a 3x3 turnaround sheet

**Approval:** images approved on (date) · rejected versions kept in `refs/_rejected/` with a reason
