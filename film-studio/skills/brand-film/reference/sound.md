# Soundtrack

**Always:** the video model makes sound effects only; music goes on in the mix where it can be changed; finish at -14 LUFS (true peak -1).

## With a voice-over: the music serves the voice

- Write and record the voice first (`voice-audition`). The emotion lives in the words; the music sets the temperature.
- Sparse under every line (piano, held chords, no busy melody), dipped about 6 dB while the voice speaks.
- Swell on the hero moment *between* lines; resolve on the logo.
- **Captions always** (most people watch on mute): short phrases timed to the words, clear of the platform's buttons (on 9:16, nothing in the bottom 380 px or right 180 px), never over the logo; skip a caption the end card already says.
- Ask for the music with a composition plan in sections matched to the cut, e.g. "Morning: sparse felt piano, 7.2 s / Reveal: strings bloom on the first beat, 6.6 s / Resolve: warm chord, ring out, 3.7 s".

## Without a voice-over: the music is the script

- Make or pick the track **first**: mood, BPM, length, and the drop time.
- Structure in bars: intro (anticipation), build (the details), **the drop on the reveal**, ring-out under the logo.
- Cut the picture to the beat: every cut on a beat, every big move on a bar.
- Effects punch through on the hits (the ignition, the door, the lights). If the generated track's drop is soft, make it in the mix: hold the music low through the build and lift it hard on the reveal, compress lightly so the build isn't silent on a phone.
