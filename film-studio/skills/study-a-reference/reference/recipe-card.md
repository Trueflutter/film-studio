# Technique card

One card per technique, in `00_brief/reference/STUDY.md`.

- **Name:** a plain name, e.g. "headline slam" or "flash cut on the beat".
- **Where:** the times in the reference, e.g. 0:02.40 to 0:03.10, and the strip that shows it.
- **What happens:** one or two sentences a non-editor understands.
- **Numbers:** duration, ease and overshoot (`measure curve`); size and position on screen; colours (`measure look --grid`); shot lengths (`measure cuts`).
- **How we build it:** the skill and the method: a `/hyperframes-registry` block, hand-built HyperFrames with the measured ease, a Seedance shot (`seedance-directing`), a cut in the edit, or a cue in the mix.
- **Cost:** free when it is code or the edit; otherwise the generation estimate.
- **Ours:** how it looks with the director's brand, words and colours, never the reference's.
