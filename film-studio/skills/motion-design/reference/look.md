# The look

## Brand file first

Keep the brand in `brand.md` at the studio root (or the production's `02_design/`): fonts (file names), colours as tokens (surface, surface-alt, ink, secondary, muted, hairline, ONE accent), logo files (light and dark), corner radius, any rules ("sentence case", "no gradients"). Copy fonts and logos into the composition's `assets/`. No brand? Propose one in three lines (a display face, a text face, one accent) and get an OK.

## Banned (the template look)

- A centred title on a gradient; everything fading in; bouncy type; glows, neon, lens flares; particle bursts; drop-shadow cards; frame borders and corner labels; uppercase letter-spaced eyebrows; two accents fighting; emoji as icons; stock "AI brain" or circuit imagery; crossfades; 3D for no reason; text in the corners.
- Clip-art icons. Use simple line icons at one stroke weight, or none.
- Generated text of any kind.

The test for every frame: would this look at home in a premium news explainer or an Apple keynote, or does it look like a template? If template, redo it.

## Type scale

| | 16:9 (1920×1080) | 9:16 (1080×1920) |
|---|---|---|
| Hero number or headline | 120 to 180 px | 110 to 150 px |
| Headline | 72 to 96 px | 72 to 88 px |
| Body / labels | 36 to 44 px | 40 to 48 px |
| Smallest anything | 28 px | 40 px |

Headlines tight (letter-spacing about -0.02em), one line where possible, sentence case. Numbers in tabular figures.

## Layout and safe zones

- **9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts):** keep text out of the top 220 px, the bottom 380 px and the right 180 px (the platform's buttons). Left-align in a column from x = 80.
- **16:9 (YouTube):** title-safe margin 96 px all round; lower thirds sit above the bottom 120 px.
- Over the presenter: graphics go on the side she isn't facing, never over the face or mouth; split screens give her at least 40% of the width.
- One thing first: a clear reading order; the call to action last.
- Every full-screen scene breathes: generous margins, one focal element, a slow drift.

## Slides inserted into a video

A slide in a video is a designed scene, not a screenshot of PowerPoint: one headline, at most three short lines or one chart, built in the composition with the brand. Lines arrive on the words that say them. If the user hands you a real deck, rebuild its content in code, keeping their numbers and wording.
