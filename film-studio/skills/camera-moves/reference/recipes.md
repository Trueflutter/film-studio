# Recipes

Shot plans that have worked in this course. Adapt the subject; keep the structure.

## Presenter clips (3 to 5 lip-synced clips, one setting)

| Clip | Opens on | Move | Ends on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open | three-quarter medium close-up from her left | slow arc to the front + push-in | close-up, eyes on the lens |
| Section 2 | wide, from the side of the desk, low | slow lateral track past the glass | medium shot, she turns to the lens |
| Section 3 | front close-up | locked off, then a slow push-in on the key line | tighter close-up |
| Close | medium shot from the front | slow pull out | medium wide, she holds a warm look |

Each prompt: the reference pack (face close-up + the pack shot nearest the opening framing), "she is already speaking on camera as the shot begins", one move, "her mouth closes after the last word and she holds the look", no music, no text.

## Multi-angle B-roll take (10 to 15 s, 4 or 5 shots)

```text
STYLE: Real footage, filmed on a cinema camera, natural colour, not CGI. A multi-angle sequence with hard cuts.
0-3s: wide aerial descending slowly toward [place], low sun between the buildings.
3-6s: hard cut to a handheld close-up tracking [hands doing the action].
6-9s: hard cut to a low-angle tracking shot moving beside [subject] at walking pace.
9-12s: hard cut to a wide from the far end: [the payoff]; the camera settles and holds.
CONSTRAINTS: hard cuts at 3, 6 and 9 s; no text, no readable signs or screens, no logos; nobody speaks; no music.
```

Every shot a different size and a different move. In the edit, use each shot **inside** its cut points.

## The one-take brand film (10 to 15 s, no cuts)

The oner is a chain of causes: each beat hands the camera to the next subject.

```text
STYLE: Real [category] commercial footage, shot on a cinema camera, vertical 9:16. ONE CONTINUOUS TAKE WITH NO CUTS: a single unbroken camera move through many angles. [Energy words]. [Look: light, colour, texture].
PREMISE: [one sentence: where the camera starts and where it ends up].
[THE HERO]: [exact description, the same throughout, no badges or logos].
[THE PLACE]: [what's in it, materials, light; no signs with words].
0-3s: already moving on the first frame: [start framing and move, the cause]. One continuous take.
3-6s: still the same unbroken move, [the hand-off: a whip or a turn following the cause], no cut.
6-11s: without cutting, [the journey through the place: weave, sweep, slide past named things]. No cut.
11-15s: still the same take, [the reveal: rise or pull back], then the camera settles and holds for the last second.
SOUND: [score feel, the hero's sounds, the hit on the reveal]. No voices.
CONSTRAINTS: one continuous take from the first frame to the last: no cuts, no fades. [The hero] throughout. Strictly no subtitles, captions, on-screen text, logos, badges or readable signage. Nobody speaks. Real photography, not CGI.
```

Proven on VIP Motors (Oct 2026): every timestamped beat landed, no cuts. Graphics and the real logo go on afterwards in code.

## Product hero (5 to 8 s)

Macro slide across the material → hard cut to a slow orbit (once) on the product on its surface → hard cut to a pull out that settles on the product with space for the end card. Real reflections, one light source named.

## Property walkthrough (10 to 15 s)

Aerial descend toward the building at golden hour → hard cut to a glide through the open front door into the living room → the glide continues to the window and the water view, settles → hard cut to a slow pull out on the balcony at dusk. Empty rooms unless the brief has people; never a named landmark in the skyline.

## Food or process (8 to 12 s)

Top-down as the hands set out the ingredients → hard cut to a macro slide across the texture → hard cut to a side-on locked-off shot of the pour or the cut → hard cut to a slow push-in on the finished thing.
