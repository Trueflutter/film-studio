# The moves

Each move: what it says, when to use it, wording that Seedance 2.5 performs, and what to avoid. Speeds: slow (a face, a reveal), steady (a walk, a glide), fast (a car, a transition), snapping (a whip).

| Move | It says | Use it for | Write it like | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Push in** | getting closer to the truth | a realisation, a key line, a product's detail | "the camera pushes in slowly from a medium shot to a close-up on her eyes" | pushing in on every clip; a push that never stops |
| **Pull out / reveal** | the bigger picture | establishing a place, an ending, scale | "starting on the keys in her hand, the camera pulls back steadily to reveal the empty apartment and the water beyond" | revealing a famous landmark (describe the skyline instead) |
| **Arc** | looking at it from a new side | presenter openings, a person thinking | "the camera arcs slowly from her left toward the front, ending on a close-up" | arcs over about 90 degrees on a face (the face drifts) |
| **Orbit** | the world stands still for this | a hero product, one big moment (use once) | "the camera orbits once, slowly, around the watch on the marble plinth" | orbiting a talking face; more than one orbit per film |
| **Tracking / follow** | we go with them | someone walking, a car, a runner | "a tracking shot moving beside her at walking pace as she crosses the lobby" | following from straight behind for long (we lose the face) |
| **Lead** | they are coming toward us | walking-and-talking, arrivals | "the camera backs away ahead of him as he walks toward it down the corridor" | fast leads indoors (collisions, warping walls) |
| **Truck / slide** | scanning a line of things | a row of products, a shelf, a skyline | "the camera slides sideways past the row of bottles, each one catching the light" | sliding while also zooming |
| **Crane up / rise** | leaving, a verdict, scale | endings, the final wide | "the camera rises in one smooth crane move, revealing the whole showroom, then settles" | rising with nothing new revealed |
| **Crane down / descend** | arriving | openings, entering a scene | "the camera descends from above the palms to eye level as the car pulls in" | descending onto faces from straight above |
| **Pan** | attention shifting | following an action, connecting two things | "the camera pans with her hand from the cup to the open laptop" | slow pans across empty space |
| **Whip pan** | sudden change, energy | transitions, comedy, sport | "a fast whip pan to the door as it bursts open" | more than one per take; whips on faces |
| **Tilt** | from small to big, or up to a reveal | a tower, a person top to toe | "the camera tilts up from the entrance to the top of the tower at dusk" | tilting and panning at once |
| **Rack focus** | look here now | foreground to background shift | "focus shifts from the glass in the foreground to her face behind it" | racking in a moving shot |
| **Handheld** | we're in it, it's real | UGC, tension, documentary | "handheld, a gentle natural sway, following at arm's length" | heavy shake (reads as broken) |
| **Glide (Steadicam)** | effortless, premium | through doorways, hotel and property walkthroughs | "a smooth glide through the open doorway into the living room, toward the window" | gliding through closed doors or walls |
| **FPV drone** | speed, thrill | cars, cities, sport, oners | "an FPV drone dive between the towers, skimming low over the water" | FPV around people's faces |
| **Aerial** | place, scale | establishing, property, travel | "a high aerial that descends slowly toward the waterfront towers at golden hour" | named landmarks; top-down too long |
| **Top-down** | order, process | food, desks, hands at work | "top-down shot of the table; her hands lay out the plates" | faces from above |
| **Dolly zoom** | unease, a realisation | one dramatic beat | "a dolly zoom: the camera pushes in while the background stretches away behind him" | anything calm; more than once |
| **Crash zoom** | shock, a punchline | comedy, reveals | "a sudden crash zoom onto the price tag" | on a generated face (warps) |
| **Macro slide** | craft, texture | product details, food, materials | "macro close-up sliding slowly across the stitching of the leather" | macro with fast movement |
| **Locked off** | stillness, attention | dialogue, a held reaction, a statement | "a locked-off camera, completely still" | locking off a long empty shot |

## Combinations that work

- **Arc + push-in** (slow): the best presenter opening.
- **Pull out + rise**: the classic ending reveal.
- **Track + whip to the next subject**: the hand-off inside a oner.
- **Glide through a doorway + settle on the window**: property and hotel.
- **Descend + land at eye level**: arriving somewhere.

## What fails

- Three moves in one beat ("pans, zooms and orbits"): the model picks one, or warps.
- "Cinematic camera movement" with no move named: you get a floating drift.
- Fast moves on faces, dialogue or hands doing something precise.
- Moves with no end framing: they drift past the subject.
- The same opening move on every clip: it's the most common amateur tell after same-angle cuts.
