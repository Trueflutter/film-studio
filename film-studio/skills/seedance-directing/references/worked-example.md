# Worked example: a four-shot sequence

An original example in the full anatomy: 9:16, 8 seconds, four shots of uneven length with cuts on whole seconds, one character, a small real emotion. A shorter three-shot version of this scene was A/B tested: `tested-example.md`. Written for Seedance 2.5 `reference-to-video` (on `image-to-video`, @Image1 becomes the start frame and the other references move into the prompt as text).

**Reference stack (4 files):**
- `@Image1` the composition of shot 1, Leila's face painted out as a white oval.
- `@Image2` Leila's face close-up, three-quarter, neutral grey, flat light.
- `@Image3` Leila's wardrobe, front and back, head cropped out.
- `@Image4` the bakery front and the street, empty, at dawn.

```
SCENE CONTEXT: A grounded slice-of-life moment at a small neighbourhood bakery at dawn. Leila, the owner, unlocks the front door expecting an empty street and finds a queue of her regulars already waiting in the cold. Small, real surprise turning into quiet pride. Four shots, photoreal, observational handheld, joined by HARD CUTS.
OUTPUT: 9:16, 8 seconds, 4 shots of 2 s, 2 s, 1 s and 3 s, hard cuts only, 24fps, real-time motion. NO MUSIC: no score, no soundtrack, no background music, no musical tones or beats of any kind. No subtitles, no on-screen text.
ACTIVE REFERENCES:
@Image1: the composition of shot 1 only: Leila from behind her LEFT shoulder at the glass door. Her face is painted out as a plain white oval: a placeholder mask, never rendered. Never use it for shots 2, 3 or 4.
@Image2: Leila's face. FACE REFERENCE ONLY: a woman in her early forties with warm brown skin, faint lines at the eyes, a small scar through her right eyebrow, dark hair pulled back with loose strands at the temples. Use it only for her face, skin, eyes and hair. Never use it for composition, framing, camera angle, lens, pose, head turn or expression.
@Image3: Leila's clothes: a flour-dusted navy apron over a grey knit jumper, sleeves pushed to the elbows. Use it only for her clothing. Never for pose, background or framing.
@Image4: the bakery front and street at dawn, empty. Use it only for the setting, materials and light. Never copy its framing.
SPATIAL MAP: Outside, facing the shop, the door is on the LEFT and the queue stands along the window on the RIGHT. Inside, facing the street, this mirrors: the door is on the RIGHT of frame and the window with the queue behind the glass is on the LEFT. She unlocks with her RIGHT hand. The queue never changes sides.
THE CAMERA: an observational documentary camera on the shoulder of an unseen operator inside the shop: a small breathing sway, a short late reframe each time she moves, never a smooth glide, never a zoom. Faces always THREE-QUARTER, never front-on, never a perfect profile.
THE PERFORMANCE: Leila starts tired and on autopilot, her hands doing a routine they know. The surprise is small and real and lives in her EYES and BROWS; the pride arrives slowly, lopsided, in one corner of the mouth. She never gasps, never covers her mouth, never looks at the camera.

SHOT 1 (0:00 to 0:02)
OPTICS: 35mm look, moderate depth of field, the street soft through the glass.
CAMERA: MEDIUM CLOSE-UP from just behind her LEFT shoulder, slightly low, the door glass in the RIGHT half of frame; the operator leans with her as she pulls.
ACTION: the key is already turning in the first frame; the bolt slides; she pulls the door inward with her shoulder.
TIMING: 0-0.7s the key turns; 0.7-1.3s the bolt clacks back; 1.3-2.0s the door swings in and cold air lifts the loose strands at her temple. THE SHOT ENDS mid-swing, on the cut.
AUDIO: the key in the lock, a heavy metal bolt, the door seal unsticking, distant traffic.
LOCK: over her shoulder; her face is not seen in this shot.

SHOT 2 (0:02 to 0:04)
OPTICS: 85mm look, shallow depth of field.
CAMERA: CLOSE-UP, THREE-QUARTER from her LEFT, at her eye height; a plain hard cut, not a zoom; the operator steps half a pace back as the door opens.
ACTION: continuing the pull from shot 1 with no pause, she looks out expecting nothing and sees the queue.
TIMING: 0-0.5s eyes still down on the handle; 0.5-0.9s her eyes lift to the street; 0.9-1.5s her brows rise a little and stay up, eyes widening slightly, lips parting a few millimetres; 1.5-2.0s one slow blink, held to the cut.
AUDIO: murmuring voices outside, one short laugh, a car passing.
LOCK: the surprise is in the eyes and brows only; her mouth stays nearly closed.

SHOT 3 (0:04 to 0:05)
OPTICS: 28mm look, deep enough to read the whole queue.
CAMERA: WIDE from outside on the wet pavement at waist height, looking back at the shop; handheld, the operator side-steps as a passer-by crosses close to the lens.
ACTION: eleven people in winter coats stand along the window on the RIGHT, two holding paper cups; an older man in a flat cap lifts one hand in a small wave. Leila stands in the doorway on the LEFT.
TIMING: 0-0.4s the passer-by crosses close to camera, dark and soft; 0.4-1.0s the older man's wave while two people at the front of the queue turn to her.
AUDIO: footsteps on the wet pavement, low chatter, someone in the queue calls "Morning!"
LOCK: Leila on the LEFT, the queue on the RIGHT; nobody looks at the camera.

SHOT 4 (0:05 to 0:08)
OPTICS: 50mm look, shallow depth of field, the queue soft through the glass behind her.
CAMERA: MEDIUM CLOSE-UP, THREE-QUARTER from her RIGHT, inside, from just beside the open door; the operator leans in by one small step during the smile.
ACTION: she breathes out through her nose, her shoulders drop, and a smile starts on one side of her mouth and slowly spreads; she gives one small shake of the head, fond and disbelieving, and pushes the door stop down with her foot.
TIMING: 0-0.8s the breath out, shoulders settling; 0.8-1.8s the smile starts at the left corner of her mouth and spreads, uneven; 1.8-2.3s one small head shake; 2.3-3.0s her foot presses the rubber door stop down; the smile holds to the last frame.
AUDIO: her breath, the door stop scraping on tile, the chatter outside rising.
LOCK: the smile is small and lopsided, teeth barely showing; she says nothing.

GLOBAL LOCK: four shots, HARD CUTS at 2, 4 and 5 seconds. Leila matches @Image2 and @Image3 in every shot; the apron and jumper never change. Screen sides follow the spatial map and never swap. No camera, crew or equipment ever appears in frame. @Image1 sets only shot 1's composition; its white oval is a placeholder mask, never rendered. The only words are one "Morning!" from the queue. NO MUSIC at all. No text, no logos, no readable signs.
LIGHTING: blue-grey dawn daylight from the street, cool on her face from the door side; warm tungsten from the ovens deep in the shop behind her, a faint warm rim on her hair and shoulder; breath visible in the cold air outside; wet pavement holding the sky.
```

**Why it reads as filmed:** a person holds the camera and reacts late; every face is three-quarter; the surprise is timed in tenths of a second and kept in the eyes; the cuts carry motion (shot 1 ends mid-swing, shot 2 picks it up); the payoff shot is the longest; every shot has its own sound; the music lock is complete. No sentence describes quality, only physical facts.

**How to run it:** draft at 480p and judge only the angles and the cut points. If they hold, harvest one frame per shot, upscale, and run shots 2 and 4 alone from their frames for the performance (two or three passes each). Use the coverage take for shots 1 and 3 if they already work.
