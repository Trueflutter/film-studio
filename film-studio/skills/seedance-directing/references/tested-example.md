# Tested example: the A/B prompt that won

Seedance 2.5 text-to-video, 9:16, 6 s, sound on, no references. Run against a 205-word prompt of the same scene written the way short prompts usually are (31 October 2026, 5 takes on EvoLink and Aristotto).

**Result:** in all three takes from this prompt the character was three-quarter or in profile, looking at the queue she was reacting to, framed off-centre in light from the street, with a small smile that built after an exhale. In both short-prompt takes she was front-on, centred, looking into the lens, and ended in a grinning presenter pose, although the short prompt said she doesn't look at the camera.

**What didn't hold:** the cuts asked for at 1.7 s and 3.6 s landed up to 0.9 s late (put cuts on whole seconds), and the apron and jumper drifted between shots with text alone (give clothes a reference image). 4,567 characters: under Aristotto's 6,000 cap.

```
SCENE CONTEXT: A grounded slice-of-life moment at a small neighbourhood bakery at dawn. Leila, the owner, unlocks the front door expecting an empty street and finds a queue of her regulars already waiting in the cold. Small, real surprise turning into quiet pride. Three shots, photoreal, observational handheld, joined by HARD CUTS.
OUTPUT: 9:16, 6 seconds, 3 shots of 1.7 s, 1.9 s and 2.4 s, hard cuts only, 24fps, real-time motion. NO MUSIC: no score, no soundtrack, no background music, no musical tones or beats of any kind. No subtitles, no on-screen text.
LEILA: a woman in her early forties with warm brown skin, faint lines at the eyes, a small scar through her right eyebrow, dark hair pulled back with loose strands at the temples; a flour-dusted navy apron over a grey knit jumper, sleeves pushed to the elbows.
SPATIAL MAP: from inside the shop facing the street, the glass door is on the RIGHT of frame and the shop window, with the queue standing outside it, is on the LEFT. She unlocks with her RIGHT hand. The queue never changes sides.
THE CAMERA: an observational documentary camera held on the shoulder of an unseen operator inside the shop: a small breathing sway, a short late reframe each time she moves, never a smooth glide, never a zoom. Her face always THREE-QUARTER, never front-on, never a perfect profile.
THE PERFORMANCE: Leila starts tired and on autopilot, her hands doing a routine they know. The surprise is small and real and lives in her EYES and BROWS; the pride arrives slowly, lopsided, in one corner of her mouth. She never gasps, never covers her mouth, never looks at the camera, and says nothing.

SHOT 1 (0:00 to 0:01.7)
OPTICS: 35mm look, moderate depth of field, the street soft through the glass.
CAMERA: MEDIUM CLOSE-UP from just behind her LEFT shoulder, slightly low, the door glass in the RIGHT half of frame; the operator leans with her as she pulls.
ACTION: the key is already turning in the first frame; the bolt slides back; she pulls the door inward with her shoulder. Through the window on the LEFT, a line of people in winter coats is soft and out of focus.
TIMING: 0-0.6s the key turns; 0.6-1.1s the bolt clacks back; 1.1-1.7s the door swings in and cold air lifts the loose strands at her temple. THE SHOT ENDS mid-swing, on the cut.
AUDIO: the key in the lock, a heavy metal bolt, the door seal unsticking, distant traffic.
LOCK: over her shoulder; her face is not seen in this shot.

SHOT 2 (0:01.7 to 0:03.6)
OPTICS: 85mm look, shallow depth of field.
CAMERA: CLOSE-UP, THREE-QUARTER from her LEFT, at her eye height; a plain hard cut, not a zoom; the operator steps half a pace back as the door opens.
ACTION: continuing the pull from shot 1 with no pause, she looks out expecting nothing and sees the queue.
TIMING: 0-0.5s eyes still down on the handle; 0.5-0.9s her eyes lift to the street; 0.9-1.5s her brows rise a little and stay up, eyes widening slightly, lips parting a few millimetres; 1.5-1.9s one slow blink, held to the cut.
AUDIO: murmuring voices outside, one short laugh, a car passing.
LOCK: the surprise is in the eyes and brows only; her mouth stays nearly closed.

SHOT 3 (0:03.6 to 0:06.0)
OPTICS: 50mm look, shallow depth of field, the warm shop interior soft behind her.
CAMERA: MEDIUM CLOSE-UP, THREE-QUARTER from her RIGHT, from just beside the open door; the operator leans in by one small step during the smile.
ACTION: she breathes out through her nose, her shoulders drop, and a smile starts on one side of her mouth and slowly spreads; she gives one small shake of the head and pushes the rubber door stop down with her foot.
TIMING: 0-0.6s the breath out, shoulders settling; 0.6-1.4s the smile starts at the left corner of her mouth and spreads, uneven; 1.4-1.8s one small head shake; 1.8-2.4s her foot presses the door stop down; the smile holds to the last frame.
AUDIO: her breath, the door stop scraping on tile, the chatter outside rising.
LOCK: the smile is small and lopsided, teeth barely showing.

GLOBAL LOCK: three shots, HARD CUTS at 1.7 and 3.6 seconds. Leila looks the same in every shot; the apron and jumper never change. Screen sides follow the spatial map and never swap. No camera, crew or equipment ever appears in frame. Nobody speaks. NO MUSIC at all. No text, no logos, no readable signs.
LIGHTING: blue-grey dawn daylight from the street, cool on her face from the door side; warm tungsten from the ovens deep in the shop behind her, a faint warm rim on her hair and shoulder; breath visible in the cold air outside; wet pavement holding the sky.
```
