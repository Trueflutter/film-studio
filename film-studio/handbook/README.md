# The Studio Handbook

Eighteen chapters you keep after the course. Your Claude reads them too: the film-studio skills open the relevant chapter before advising.

| # | Chapter | Read it when |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [The story engine](01-story-engine.md) | you have an idea, a brief, a script, or an ad to crack |
| 02 | [Visual storytelling](02-visual-storytelling.md) | you're about to write a line of exposition or explain a feeling |
| 03 | [Directing the shot](03-directing-the-shot.md) | before every shot list and prompt |
| 04 | [Realism: the 12 levers](04-realism.md) | a shot looks "AI" and you don't know why |
| 05 | [Continuity](05-continuity.md) | characters, rooms or light drift between shots |
| 06 | [Generation craft](06-generation-craft.md) | choosing a mode or engine, writing prompts, multishot, chaining |
| 07 | [Performance and dialogue](07-performance-and-dialogue.md) | anyone speaks, or a face must carry the moment |
| 08 | [Sound](08-sound.md) | always; and before you pick music |
| 09 | [Editing and finishing](09-editing-and-finishing.md) | building the cut, fixing in post, the grade |
| 10 | [Planning and previs](10-planning-and-previs.md) | shot lists, boards, animatics, 3D blockouts, render-to-real |
| 11 | [Hybrid filmmaking](11-hybrid-filmmaking.md) | real footage meets generated footage |
| 12 | [Diagnosis and iteration](12-diagnosis-and-iteration.md) | a take failed and you're tempted to re-roll |
| 13 | [Economics](13-economics.md) | before you spend, and after every session |
| 14 | [QC and delivery](14-qc-and-delivery.md) | before anything leaves your hands |
| 15 | [Rights and ethics](15-rights-and-ethics.md) | real people, voices, music, disclosure, client work |
| 16 | [Tool landscape](16-tool-landscape.md) | choosing tools (dated: re-check monthly) |
| 17 | [Case studies](17-case-studies.md) | learning from finished work, and doing your own teardowns |
| 18 | [Short-form, series and volume](18-short-form-series-and-volume.md) | reels and TikToks, scenario videos, tutorials, series, one idea in many formats, a week of content |

Three threads run through all of it: **realism** (the 12 levers, ch. 04), **economics** (still → draft → lock → final → fix, ch. 13) and **diagnosis** (name the failure, fix it cheaply, ch. 12).
